Based on the provided sources, the primary difference between Streaming Materialized Views (SMVs) and Continuous Materialized Views (CMVs) lies in whether they are optimized for low-latency updates or high-throughput data ingestion.
Here is a detailed breakdown of how they compare:
Streaming Materialized Views (SMVs)
Focus: SMVs prioritize low latency, updating in near-real-time (sub-second to low-second update times) as the source data changes
.
Processing Pattern: They process data event-by-event or via micro-batches with minimal buffering
.
Best For: Operational workloads that require immediately synchronized data, such as live dashboards, real-time analytics, and customer-facing applications
.
Example: An order dashboard that updates instantly as new transactions complete
.
Continuous Materialized Views (CMVs)
Focus: CMVs prioritize throughput and write efficiency for processing massive volumes of data, accepting eventual consistency (update times ranging from seconds to minutes)
.
Processing Pattern: They utilize larger batches optimized for efficient writes to the database rather than immediate updates
.
Best For: Large-scale ingestion tasks, bulk ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) pipelines, and data warehouse synchronization
.
Example: Nightly rollups of millions of IoT sensor events into summary tables
.

. ASP uses a unified processing engine where a single pipeline definition can serve either workload
